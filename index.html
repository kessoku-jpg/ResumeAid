How it works

01

Upload

Drop your resume as a PDF or DOCX. We pull out your skills, experience and education, and flag anything that's hard to read.

02

Analyse

We search PhilJobNet, OnlineJobs.ph and Adzuna for roles that fit, then compare each listing's meaning with your resume using Sentence-BERT.

03

Match

See the roles ranked by score, with a breakdown for skills, experience and job title, plus the keywords you share.
